THE HUMAN BEHIND SPACE4JOY

A journey shaped by people, purpose and possibility.

I'm a farmer's daughter from a small village on the slopes of Mount Kilimanjaro, Tanzania, where I grew up surrounded by the rhythms of farm life, community and connection. I came to Canada in 2008 through a scholarship, carrying big dreams, a hymn book and a deep sense of possibility. Carleton University became my first home in Canada, and I went on to earn a Master's degree in Political Science.

My professional journey has taken me across sectors, cultures and continents. Over more than 20 years, I've worked in international development, community building, public policy, program development, social innovation and leadership. I've collaborated with people and organizations in more than 20 countries and worked across very different contexts, always learning from the people, relationships and experiences that shape the work.

My work and interests have been grounded in human-centred design, change management, learning, collaboration and the belief that meaningful progress happens when we pay attention to people, not just plans and outcomes.

A DIFFERENT UNDERSTANDING OF SUCCESS

Success became more than what I could achieve.

But my understanding of success has also been shaped by life beyond work.

I'm a mother, a lifelong learner and someone who has experienced the pull of ambition, the demands of responsibility and the cost of trying to do it all. For a long time, I measured success by what I could achieve, how much I could take on and how far I could go. Then came a season when the demands of work, home and personal ambition caught up with me. I reached a point where I had to question not only what I was pursuing, but what I was sacrificing in the process.

That became the beginning of a seven-year transformation in how I understood success. I didn't want to give up on ambition. I wanted a different relationship with it. I wanted to build a life where achievement and fulfilment could coexist, where I could care for others without continually abandoning myself, and where JOY had a place in the everyday, not just at the end of a long list of accomplishments.

WHY SPACE4JOY?

That shift inspired SPACE4JOY.

Today, I bring together my lived experiences, professional background and passion for human connection to create practical resources and experiences that help people navigate stress, reconnect with what matters and cultivate JOY across contexts, stages and seasons of life.

I believe you are more than your job, your title, your responsibilities or your achievements. I believe the people behind the work matter. And I believe you deserve success you can enjoy and a life you don't need to escape from.
